# CASE 013: THE 13TH ROOM
### A First-Person Paranormal Detective Mystery & Psychological Horror Game
*Frozen in Rural Rajasthan, India — July 1998 and the Present Night*

---

## 🕯️ GAME OVERVIEW
**CASE 013: THE 13TH ROOM** is a first-person paranormal investigation and detective mystery set inside the 300-year-old Rathore Haveli in rural Rajasthan. 

On **13 July 1998 at 2:13 AM**, seventeen-year-old Meera Rathore disappeared during a violent monsoon storm. Her family claimed she ran away. The local police closed the file. But every clock in the haveli stopped at 2:13 AM, a 13th bell tolled in a courtyard built for twelve, and the architectural plans had an entire room erased from the blueprints.

Twenty-eight years later, you enter the haveli as an independent paranormal investigator with an audio cassette containing thirteen knocks and a chilling whisper:
> *"You counted twelve rooms because you were never supposed to find the thirteenth."*

## 🔍 UNIQUE GAMEPLAY MECHANICS

### 1. 3-Layer Evidence System
Every major clue discovered inside the haveli features three distinct investigative layers:
- **Layer 1 (Physical Evidence):** Tangible artifacts (the frozen 2:13 clock, the signed 3:00 AM renovation order, Meera's calfskin diary, the Rajput brass key).
- **Layer 2 (Documentary Record):** Official records, police files, and architectural notes that contradict the family's public narrative.
- **Layer 3 (Paranormal Anomaly):** Impossible phenomena that defy conventional physics (clocks ticking backwards, wet ink appearing in a 28-year-old diary describing the player, spectral figures).

### 2. Interactive Red-Yarn Evidence Board
- Pinned polaroids and documents on an authentic corkboard.
- Connect contradictory clue pairs with red string to deduce key breakthroughs:
  - *The Fabricated Flight* (Clue 1 + Clue 7)
  - *The Silenced Witness* (Clue 4 + Clue 1)
  - *The Architectural Concealment* (Clue 3 + Clue 9)
  - *The Housekeeper's Secret Key* (Clue 2 + Clue 8)
  - *The 1963 Erased Child* (Clue 5 + Clue 11)
  - *The Temporal Anomaly* (Clue 6 + Clue 13)

### 3. Forensic Audio Demodulation
- Play the cassette tape to hear Meera's voice and the 13 knocks.
- Switch the **Spectral Demodulator (150Hz Low-Pass)** to strip the modern splice and isolate the erased child's voice:
  > *"...Devendra erased my name... 1963... Anand... they walled me behind the shrine..."*

### 4. Dynamic Lantern & Hidden Geometry
- The 13th chamber does not exist under normal daylight. Bringing your flickering kerosene lantern within 4 meters of the cracked eastern corridor reveals the pulsing occult Rajasthani mandala etched into the mortar.
- Use the **Brass Key with Housekeeper Prints** in Chapter 4 to breach the sealed chamber.

### 5. Suspect Interrogation & Contradiction System
Examine 5 suspects:
1. **Thakur Vikram Singh Rathore** *(The Patriarch)*
2. **Kamla Bai** *(The Housekeeper)*
3. **Arvind Joshi** *(The Architectural Historian)*
4. **Inspector Devendra Rathore** *(The Retired Investigator)*
5. **Naina Rathore** *(The Last Descendant)*
Challenge their alibis when you have collected the required physical contradiction!

## 📜 STORY CHAPTERS
- **Chapter 1: The Gate That Closed** — Enter the haveli, examine the 1998 ancestral portrait, and retrieve the floor plan.
- **Chapter 2: Twelve Doors** — Search the family shrine, recover Meera's journal, and investigate the stopped grandfather clock.
- **Chapter 3: The Five Names** — Piece together the evidence board, demodulate the audio tape, and break Inspector Devendra's testimony.
- **Chapter 4: The Wall That Breathes** — Uncover Arvind's measurements and use the brass key to reveal the hidden 13th room.
- **Chapter 5: 2:13 AM** — Enter the frozen room, uncover the 1963 birth talisman, and decide the fate of Case 013.

## 🚪 THE FOUR BRANCHING ENDINGS
- **Ending A: The Truth Is Recorded** *(Transmit the full forensic dossier to the Rajasthan authorities; at dawn the wall dissolves, but in the final photo the extra figure now stands behind you).*
- **Ending B: The Family Secret** *(Burn the paranormal records to protect Naina and Kamla; the crime is settled privately, but your case file now holds 13 photos instead of 12).*
- **Ending C: The Thirteenth Entry** *(Rush into the room without solving the deductions; the heavy door locks shut and your name is written in wet ink into Meera's journal as Case 014 opens).*
- **Secret Ending: The Room Was Never Empty** *(Unlocked by discovering all 13 clues, solving all 6 deductions, and listening to the demodulated audio in Room 13: the room predates both disappearances, and a second shadow waves beside yours).*
